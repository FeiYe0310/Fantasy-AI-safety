# V24 — Giant Olive World

## What Pear's structure teaches us

The fruit is not a prop that appears in each scene. It is the stage, camera anchor, transition surface, and recurring proof of continuity. Every chapter must preserve one recognisable part of the same object while changing scale or viewpoint.

For this site, the olive occupies at least 50% of visual attention in every chapter. Nüwa becomes the small intelligence acting on that world. Text receives a dedicated quiet zone opposite the object and alternates sides rather than covering the focal subject.

## Continuous camera grammar

1. **Giant fruit:** the olive groove aligns with the celestial crack.
2. **Pit:** the camera follows the groove downward; the fruit becomes a monumental pit embedded in soil.
3. **Roots:** the camera enters the opened pit; five roots become an underground evidence cathedral.
4. **Tree:** the central root carries the camera upward and becomes the colossal trunk.
5. **Chosen leaf:** the camera orbits a branch and lands on one enormous olive leaf.
6. **Proof:** the camera moves around the same leaf, revealing five traceable veins on its underside.
7. **Repair:** the leaf rises without cutting away; its tip and veins join the celestial fissure.
8. **Peace:** the camera follows the healed seam down to a monumental olive branch and a white dove.

## Shared image prompt rules

Restrained neoclassical oil painting with Chinese landscape sensibility; monumental surreal scale; broad blended brushwork, sfumato, and luminous glazing. The olive form is instantly recognisable at thumbnail size. Nüwa remains tiny, back-facing or indistinct, in ivory robe with pale jade trim and muted cinnabar sash. No clear portrait, no human skin scales, no ruler-like waist tool, no neon rainbow, no glossy 3D, no game-ad fantasy art, no text, no watermark.
