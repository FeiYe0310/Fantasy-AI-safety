# V25 — The Olive That Fell from Heaven

## Narrative spine

The sky forgets how to hold itself together. A single olive falls through the fracture. Nüwa receives it, plants its pit, grows five independent roots of verification, raises a tree whose safeguards scale with capability, selects a traceable leaf, and uses that living evidence to mend the sky. Only then does the olive leaf become a symbol of peace.

The olive is never decoration. It changes state while remaining the same narrative object:

`sky wound → falling olive → received olive → pit → roots → tree → leaf → evidence → repair → peace`

## Shared visual rules

- Very wide 2.16:1 cinematic composition for full-viewport scroll scenes.
- Luminous neoclassical oil painting blended with refined Chinese landscape painting.
- Clear blue, ivory, jade-olive, restrained cinnabar, and warm gold.
- Broad, smooth painterly modelling; no dry cracked skin, scales, tile-like texture, or dense micro-impasto.
- Nüwa has long unbound black hair and layered ivory hanfu with jade and cinnabar accents. Her face remains indistinct and integrated into the environment.
- Nüwa is shown as a complete connected figure. Never show isolated hands. Hands must be anatomically coherent and separated from one another.
- The olive or its transformed state occupies at least half of the visual attention in every chapter.
- Leave a deliberately calm, darker text-safe region on the indicated side.
- No text, letters, logos, borders, or watermark inside the artwork.

## New opening keyframes

### 01 — Heaven breaks · text left

A vast mythic Chinese mountain-and-river world at luminous dawn. The blue sky tears open in one enormous diagonal ivory-gold fissure on the upper right. Nüwa stands as a small complete back-facing figure on a lower-right ridge, looking up. No olive is visible yet. Preserve the left 38 percent as calm darker blue atmospheric negative space.

### 02 — The olive falls · text left

The immediate next moment from the same camera axis. One single giant jade-green olive falls from the celestial fracture, suspended center-right with a restrained trail of luminous particles. Its groove echoes the sky wound. Nüwa remains a small complete figure on the lower-right ridge, preparing to receive it. No branch or tree exists.

### 03 — Nüwa receives it · text right

The camera has swept closer and around the same olive. Nüwa is a complete three-quarter figure at left-center, cradling the single torso-sized olive in two open palms held close together but clearly separate. Her face is soft and indistinct. Preserve the right 34 percent as calm darker sky for copy. No ruler or measuring tool at her waist.
